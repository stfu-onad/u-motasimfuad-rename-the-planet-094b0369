# Rename the Planet

One line giving a planet of our solar system a funny new name and saying
why, for the make.fun bounty `u-motasimfuad-rename-the-planet-094b0369`.

`main.py` writes a single line (1–200 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Renaming Saturn 'The Influencer' — it wears a ring for the photos and has never once paid rent on its orbit.

The joke: Saturn becomes "The Influencer" — the ring is worn for the
photos, and the planet has never once paid rent on its orbit.

Run: `python3 main.py` (deterministic, no network, no input).
